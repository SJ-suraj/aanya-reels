Aanya is a **disclosed AI persona**: an AI-generated character. The images keep the AI
provenance the image model embeds, every Buffer post is flagged `isAiGenerated: true`, and
the account bio says she is an AI creator. Nothing in this run hides that.

Follow every step in order. Each step is gated on the one before. "Abort" means: stop,
call nothing else that spends or posts, and write the final message with the reason.
**Never call Buffer `create_post` after an abort.**

## 3. Generate the plate (0.2)
Topview `topview_generate_image` `{taskType: "text_to_image", model: "GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst",
prompt: <PLATE prompt>, aspectRatio: "4:5", resolution: "1K", quality: "medium",
generateCount: 1, boardId: "8ff914f298bd4714b942d1965b4b25ec"}`.
Poll `topview_query_task {taskType: "text_to_image", taskId, shortenUrls: false,
needCloudFrontUrl: true}` until `status` is `success` or `failed`, following HOW TO WAIT below.
On success take `images[0].fileId` (the plate's own file id, proven to work as a second input)
and `images[0].filePath` (the full-size PNG; with `shortenUrls: false` it is the long CloudFront
URL, not a short api.topview.ai link).

HOW TO WAIT (applies to every poll in steps 3 and 4). The sandbox blocks any bare `sleep`
longer than about 20 seconds, and stacking background sleeps loses track of time. Do exactly
this instead:
- Right after each submit, run Bash `date -u +%s` once and keep the number as START.
- Between polls, wait with this one Bash command and nothing else:
  `i=0; until [ $i -ge 10 ]; do sleep 2; i=$((i+1)); done; date -u +%s`
  (about 20 seconds; it prints NOW). Do not use `run_in_background`, do not chain sleeps.
- After each wait, poll once. Keep going while `status` is `running`.
- A task has timed out only when NOW minus START is 360 or more, as printed by those two
  `date` calls. Never declare a timeout from how many polls you have made or how long it
  feels; a task usually needs 60 to 120 seconds and sometimes more. Only a real 360-second
  timeout or `status: failed` aborts the run.
