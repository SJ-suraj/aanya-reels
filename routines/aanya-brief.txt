## 2b. Find the hook (Exa, no credits; required for 4b and 5b)
Reels of a pretty person at a pretty place with nothing to read get almost no reach on a cold
account (measured on a comparable AI travel account: 110–134 views, zero saves), while a
specific warning about the same place gets 10x the views plus saves and shares. So every Reel
opens on a burned-in headline that names one real mistake, scam, rule or timing trap at this
place, and the carousel caption's first line carries the same hook.
1. Exa `web_search_exa`: "first-hand traveller complaints (Reddit r/travel, r/solotravel,
   forums) about a specific mistake, scam or rule at <place>: exact prices, times, dates".
   Pick the one fact that is (a) specific (a number, a time, a price, a year), (b) repeated by
   at least two independent pages, (c) not already used as a headline in any earlier caption
   from the step 0 `list_posts` results. Keep the two URLs for the final message.
2. Write `HOOK` as JSON (this goes into the job file in 4b and is burned onto the Reel):
   ```
   {"place": "<PLACE · COUNTRY, upper case>",
    "headline": "<a statement, not a question; 5–9 words; names the place or its landmark; carries the number or the word 'banned', 'costs', 'closed', 'scam', 'trap' or 'ruined'>",
    "beats": ["<the mistake, one sentence, ≤ 16 words>",
              "<what it costs you, with the number, ≤ 16 words>",
              "<the fix, one concrete instruction, ≤ 18 words>"],
    "close": "<'Send this to' + who: ≤ 8 words>"}
   ```
   Examples that render well: headline "The "free" camel ride at Petra costs $70"; "You can't
   drive to Moraine Lake anymore"; "Lauterbrunnen is ruined by 10 am". Never "wait for it",
   never a question, never an emoji (the font has none), nothing false or exaggerated: every
   number comes from the pages you read.
3. The card's CAPTION line 1 is replaced by the headline (still inside 125 characters, place
   named); line 2 becomes beat 3 (the fix); line 3 stays the "send this to…" line; hashtags
   unchanged.
